# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Technical recruiters, engineering managers, tech leads, and potential clients evaluating Benjamin Droguett for Backend Developer, Software Engineer, or engineering internship roles.

## Product Purpose

Professional developer portfolio and technical showcase for Benjamin Droguett (Ingeniería Civil en Computación e Informática student). It exists to clearly communicate engineering competence, backend architecture capabilities, code quality, and structured problem-solving skills, converting profile reviews into interview opportunities and professional connections.

## Positioning

A backend-focused software engineer with rigorous computer science fundamentals (.NET, C#, Node.js, Python, APIs, Linux), communicating depth and technical craft through a fast, interactive, and bilingual web experience rather than a static resume.

## Operating Context

Evaluated in fast-paced hiring reviews and technical screenings across desktop and mobile devices. Visitors scan credentials in seconds before deciding whether to inspect project repos, test live demos, or initiate direct contact via email, LinkedIn, or GitHub.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack**: Astro, Tailwind CSS v4, GSAP, TypeScript.
- **Internationalization**: Full client-side bilingual support (Spanish & English) managed via `src/i18n/translations.ts`.
- **Core Sections**: Hero with interactive terminal identity, About with engineering philosophy & metrics, Filterable Projects showcase with live/GitHub links, Categorized Tech Stack, and direct Contact channels.
- **Constraints**: Maintain factual accuracy regarding current education status, skills, and project accomplishments without fabricated credentials.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: Benjamin Droguett
- **Role / Identity**: Backend Developer & Ing. Civil en Computación e Informática (en formación).
- **Tone & Voice**: Rigorous, technical, structured, pragmatic, and clear.
